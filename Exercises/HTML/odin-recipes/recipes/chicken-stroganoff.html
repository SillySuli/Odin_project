Slow Cooker Chicken Stroganoff

This chicken stroganoff is so good and so easy to make in the slow cooker on a busy day. It is a simple, creamy sauce with cubed chicken breast. Serve over hot cooked rice or pasta.

Ingredients

  • 4 skinless, bonelss chicken breast halves
  • 1/8 cup butter
  • 1 package dry Italain-style salad dressing mix
  • 1 can condensed cream of chicken soup
  • 1 package cream cheese

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Put chicken, butter, and dressing mix in slow cooker.
  3. Mix together well and cook on Low for 5 to 6 hours.
  4. Mix together well and cook on Low for 5 to 6 hours.
  5. Add condensed soup and cream cheese. Mix together and cook on High until heated through and warm, about 30 minutes.
